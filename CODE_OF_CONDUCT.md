# Code of Conduct

We want local-brain to be a project anyone can take part in — whatever their background, identity,
experience or language. Everyone who takes part, in any of the project's spaces, agrees to this.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that helps: say what would resolve it, and accept feedback the same way.
- Prefer evidence to authority — in this project, a measurement settles more than a title does.
- Make room for people writing in a second language.
- Own your mistakes and help fix them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers listed in `.github/governance.json`, through the
[private reporting form](https://github.com/honeyground-org/local-brain/security/advisories/new) (start
the title with "Conduct:"). Reports are kept confidential. A maintainer involved in the report steps
aside from handling it.

## What happens

Maintainers review every report and respond fairly. Depending on what happened: a private note, a public
warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces. Maintainers who do not follow
this code may be removed from their role.
